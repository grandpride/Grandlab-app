Why SSPay

UK bank cards weren't built for life in Malaysia.

Questions

Good to know.

Is SSPay available now?

Not yet. SSPay is in development. Join the waitlist and we'll message you when invites open.

Is SSPay a bank?

No. SSPay is an app and card. Accounts, cards and currency exchange will be provided by a partner licensed by Bank Negara Malaysia.

Who can sign up?

UK passport holders living in Malaysia, aged 18 or over. Exact eligibility is confirmed at launch.

Should I close my UK bank account?

No. SSPay works alongside it. Keep your UK account for your salary, pension and UK bills, and top up SSPay when you need ringgit.

What should I use until SSPay launches?

We've put together a setup guide for today so you can cut card fees right now.
