How to read it

Staff appear when they clock in on the Grandpride attendance app, move to the rest room while on lunch, and leave when they clock out. Cars sit in the Clean Bay (PPF, tint, wrap, coating) or the Wash & Polish Bay from the job card; the coloured tag is the job stage. Cars on the street are Ready and not collected. The lounge shows Grandpride supercars that are free to rent. Cyan visors are AI agents. Loose ends = unpaid jobs not collected after 3 days.
