How to read it

Staff walk in from the road when they clock in on the Grandpride attendance app, sit at the Rest Corner on lunch, and walk out when they clock out. Cars drive in from the job card: Car Wash, Polish & Coating (polish, fast lane vacuum, lifter), the two PPF Clean Rooms (PPF, wrap, tint), then Final Check on the driveway, and park in Ready Pickup when done. The Grandpride lounge shows the best free cars in the fleet. Glowing figures are your AI team from Agent Desk: they work in the Office, Iqa AI is at Reception, AI CEO and AI MD are in the Boss Room upstairs. When you ask the board, the agents walk up the stairs to the Boss Room and speak. Kemas mops the floors and waters the plants. Tap a PPF room to see inside (the Boss Room hides). Zoom in to see names and job titles. Loose ends = unpaid jobs not collected after 3 days. Old TV? Use Lite.
