How to read it

Staff walk in when they clock in on the Grandpride attendance app, go to the rest room on lunch, and walk out when they clock out. Cars drive into the Clean Bay (PPF, tint, wrap, coating) or the Wash & Polish Bay from the job card, and out to the street when Ready. The lounge shows the best free cars in the Grandpride fleet. Glowing figures are your AI team from Agent Desk. When you ask the board, the agents in that meeting walk to the meeting room and speak. They also they take rooftop breaks (pool, sun deck, smoke corner) and ride the lift and the slide. Kemas mops the floors and waters the plants. Zoom in to see everyone’s name and job title. Loose ends = unpaid jobs not collected after 3 days. Old TV? Use Lite.
