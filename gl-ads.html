How to read this. Return on ad spend = money paid in POS by the customers who came from that ad, divided by what Meta charged. Scale = RM3 or more back for every RM1 spent. Hold = RM1 to RM3. Cut = under RM1. Learning = under RM100 spent, too early to judge. Leads = WhatsApp chats that came from the ad (the code in the first message, or Meta's click-to-WhatsApp tag). Paid orders and revenue are matched by phone number in POS, so a customer who pays under another number is missed. Spend and ad pictures come from Meta every hour.
